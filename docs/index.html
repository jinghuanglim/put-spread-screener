What the screen does

🚦 The three gates — and what they don’t cover

Each name is tested in order. Any failure drops it — these are vetoes, not scores, so there is no "good enough on balance".

Gate 1 · trend
Price must sit above the average of the previous 20 trading days (completed days only) — however good the premium looks.
Gate 2 · earnings
No results due on or before expiry. A date inside the window risks the overnight gap this structure cannot survive. Two sources are cross-checked; a disagreement is flagged, not guessed at.
Gate 3 · news
Not automated. Headlines are printed below for you to read — a guidance cut, probe or downgrade is a veto regardless of premium.
Width filter
Strikes must sit at least $5 apart, ruling out anything trading under roughly $100.

Not covered: this does not price anything, size a position, or know what you already hold. Passing means it survived three filters, nothing more — most days, the honest answer is to do nothing.
